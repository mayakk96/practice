Fictional educational ledger controls. Every journal entry has a debit account, a credit account, one positive integer minor-unit amount and one currency. The structural journal pair produces equal total debits and credits; an unbalanced entry cannot be represented. Account currencies must agree with the journal currency. Capture, settlement and compensation have distinct journal event identities, protected by database uniqueness. Append-only history explains why balances change, unlike overwriting a balance field. A reversal creates compensating postings rather than erasing the original entry. Operational exception resolution never changes journals. This simplified ledger describes synthetic receivable, payable and clearing positions; it is not a complete bank general ledger, liquidity model or available-balance engine. Never interpret a read model or model answer as a source of truth for money movement.
