Fictional educational reconciliation runbook. Reconciliation compares immutable snapshots of internal transactions and external settlement evidence for an explicit business date and currency. The variance equals total internal minor units minus total external minor units. A positive variance means the internal signed total exceeds the external signed total; it does not prove money was lost. An amount mismatch requires checking source records, signs, rounding rules and file provenance. A missing external record may represent a delayed file; a missing internal record may indicate an unknown reference. Duplicate external rows must be investigated before resubmission. Currency and status mismatches are separate anomalies. Preserve the original run, inspect mismatch transaction IDs and import records, then rerun with corrected evidence. Resolving an operational exception records an investigation conclusion; it never rewrites the ledger or fixes a financial mismatch automatically. Avoid speculation unsupported by records.
