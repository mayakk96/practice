Fictional educational settlement policy. A settlement batch groups eligible cleared transactions for one business date and one currency. Debit and credit totals use integer minor units; never add USD, EUR and GBP together. A unique settlement-item transaction reference prevents duplicate batch membership. Batch status progresses OPEN to READY to SUBMITTED to CONFIRMED to RECONCILED. Confirmation changes eligible transactions to SETTLED and posts balanced settlement journals in one database transaction. A confirmed batch cannot be silently edited. Reconciliation compares internal positions with separately received external records; confirmation does not prove those records agree. If a variance appears, inspect the reconciliation results and transaction IDs before determining the cause. Report monetary amounts with their currency and distinguish gross debit, gross credit and net settlement. No AI assistant can confirm batches or release funds.
