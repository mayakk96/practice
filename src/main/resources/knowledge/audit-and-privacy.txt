Fictional educational privacy and audit policy, version 1.

Use fictional applicants and reserved example.com or .test email addresses only. Never enter actual customer information. Documents store only fictional metadata; no document upload or identity files are supported. Never log applicant names, email addresses, free-text reasons, document references, model questions, or credentials. Correlation identifiers connect technical events without including personal data. Onboarding status history records state transitions inside the database transaction. Kafka domain events contain only identifiers and workflow metadata. Audit consumers deduplicate by event identifier because delivery can happen more than once. MCP tools are read-only and expose sanitized case status and history without applicant details or free-text reasons.
